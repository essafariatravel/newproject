# ESSAFARIA VISA OS — SEO V1.2 design

## Goal

Make the approved public B2B portal more useful and discoverable for Algerian travel agencies looking for professional visa and eVisa processing, without exposing private offers, rates, traveller files, or staff workflows.

## Prior audit and decision

The owner approved implementation of the audit's V1.2 recommendation on 2026-11-09. Production at implementation start is commit `71014b5906cece8cfa5050137bd303f4377f4378` on `seo/agency-content-2026-11-09`. Google has accepted a sitemap of 15 public URLs but has not yet crawled or indexed most of them. Lighthouse's basic SEO audit scores 100, while the French home page has a mobile performance score of 87 and LCP of 3.6s. This work does not promise instant indexing or ranking.

## Decisions

- Preserve the five approved public pages and their EN/FR/AR localized routes, existing canonical/hreflang, sitemap, and all private `noindex` rules.
- Make eVisa terminology discoverable through accurate, restrained text on Home and B2B, without publishing a catalogue of visas/destinations, prices, promises of approval, or changing the product claims.
- Add an editorial, verifiable operator section to About (ESSAFARIA TRAVEL brand, Algiers address, IATA identifier, tourism licence, official corporate website). Do not infer an unverified registered legal suffix. Clarify IATA accreditation is for ticketing, not visa issuance. Preserve the owner's editable introductory text sourced from existing site settings.
- Include safe, minimal `Organization` JSON-LD on approved home pages in Production only, linking the B2B portal to its parent operator and established logo; never emit personal/client/private data or include the script on private/Preview pages.
- Prioritize deterministic public image payload improvements where measured: preserve the visual logo, generate an appropriately sized derivative for the display header, leave the original logo intact for brand/schema and keep aspect ratios and accessible labels. Evaluate mobile performance again before deployment; do not alter app caching or security boundaries.
- Strengthen the contextual link from the corporate site if it is editable through an authorized channel; otherwise record the dependency and leave the already-existing public links untouched.

## Approaches considered

1. **Targeted public SEO improvements (chosen)** — concentrated, low-risk, measurable; respects V1 design and preserves private business code.
2. Full public redesign — could help conversion but adds visual regression and deployment risk unrelated to today's indexing problem.
3. Large public visa/destination catalogue — could attract broader traffic but violates the owner's explicit confidentiality rules and attracts the wrong B2C audience.

## Out of scope

Database migrations/seeds, Supabase production writes, authentication, RBAC, tenant isolation, wallet, catalogue/prices, administrative features, changes to login design, paid SEO campaigns, bought links, automatic publication of consular advice, and any request to falsely guarantee a Google ranking.

## Acceptance

Test the public page copy in each language, Organization JSON-LD placement and escaping, safe Preview behavior, original security and sitemap contracts, mobile media payload, and rendered EN/FR/AR at desktop/mobile. Compare live Preview to Production and deploy Production only after a verified release gate and checks. Search Console takes its own time to reprocess changes.
